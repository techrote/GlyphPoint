Arrow keys move by cell. Shift plus arrows selects. Space paints; Shift plus Space paints the secondary glyph. Enter opens text entry. Delete clears the selection or focused cell. Home and End move within a row; Control or Command plus Home or End moves to the document edges. Use Tab to leave the field. File, Readable text opens an accessible text view.

Resize and Skew

Resize samples the source into the requested size. Skew applies horizontal then vertical shear using inverse sampling. Expand keeps the transformed bounds; large results are rejected before changing the document.
